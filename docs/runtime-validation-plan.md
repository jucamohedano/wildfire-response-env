# Incident-day runtime validation plan

Status: proposed implementation work. The report changes add visibility and
evidence; they do not execute or satisfy this new acceptance matrix.

Tracked in [issue #50](https://github.com/jucamohedano/wildfire-response-env/issues/50),
with #34 retaining source and operational-calibration follow-ups.

Do the core robustness work **now, before further scenario generation or training**. The current cases can exercise it on one CPU; otherwise additional trajectories may amplify undetected defects. Broader empirical calibration and a larger evaluation corpus can follow once this gate is trustworthy. Extend the existing environment, evaluator and tests, not a second simulator or validation framework.

## Evidence and boundaries

- **Recorded complete:** `results/realism-remediation-20261029.json` reports static compilation/screening of all 12 active development configurations (six fixture, six real-map), with no recorded contradictions, but missing land masks and synthetic physics marked unsupported; that screen ran zero policies. It also records six completed real-map 1,440-minute days with exact replay and independent reward agreement. Its automated exercise-consistency gate passed, but human review remains pending; recorded aircraft checks use replay evidence and do not directly record clearance.
- **Blocked or unresolved:** physical and operational calibration, a reviewed whole-cell land/water mask and human review are not established. The [new aerial review](../data/incident_day_v2/fuel_review/visual_land_context_v1.json) suggests land at all 27 previously unclassified sample centres, but does not establish whole-cell water fractions or historical fuel. The active cases are related development probes, not an independent training or held-out corpus. A route gap is not proof of a traversable route or of physical impossibility. Do not use held-out outcomes to choose thresholds or tune policies.
- **Not run in preparing this plan:** new tests, rollouts, replay, calibration or acceptance checks. Existing test names and recorded results indicate coverage, not a new pass for the proposed matrix.

Keep three claims distinct: **modeled correctness** means consistency with explicit simulator rules and deterministic replay; **plausibility** means a screening judgment under documented source-derived or assumed bounds, including unsupported cases; **empirical calibration** requires independently sourced observations and validation, and is not implied by either of the first two.

## Implementation, in priority order

1. **Make coverage explicit and fail closed.** Extend `eval/check_incident_day_plausibility.py` and its report to derive the expected cases and input/network pins from `wildfire_core/catalog_lifecycle.py` (`registry()` / `active_day_case_ids()`), rather than relying on a default case list. Enumerate the full 6 + 6 active cases crossed with each required policy and check; include lifecycle, implementation, scenario, profile/bounds, input and network identities, seed, horizon and policy version. Record per cell `complete`, `blocked` (with reason), or `not_run` (including timeout/interruption); never interpret missing cells as passing. A complete rollout must reach 1,440 minutes and pass `verify_status()` / `verify_episode()` exact replay and independent reward checks, plus applicable runtime checks. Keep private audits/checkpoints separate from actor observations. Extend `tests/test_catalog_lifecycle.py` and `tests/test_incident_day_plausibility.py` for missing, duplicate, wrong-pin and archived entries. Existing runner `eval/run_incident_day.py` is fixture-oriented and its real-source pack is blocked; explicitly wire the existing real-map input path rather than claiming that runner already covers both suites.

2. **Add contextual duration screens.** Extend `incident_day/plausibility.py` (`route_checks`, `audit_case`) and version `data/incident_day_v2/plausibility/bounds-v1.json` without changing historical results. For each road/walk/air leg, compare elapsed time and distance to the actual directed edge, mode, applicable speed, slope and route/closure context from `incident_day/routing.py` (`HazardRouter.options`, `safe_journey_segment`) and the existing mobility router. Check setup, travel, work, return, rest and refill durations against the applicable scenario profile and observed state transitions, accounting for minute quantization, initial inherited on-scene time, interruptions, blocked/assistance states and incomplete/censored phases. Impossible model transitions are contradictions; loose upper bounds or exceptional slowdowns are review candidates, and absent leg/timing evidence is unsupported, not a pass. Avoid treating the global maximum-road-speed displacement check as route reconstruction or imposing a universal operational limit.

   Attach units, provenance, version, applicability and uncertainty to each parameter: identify values actually present in pinned source/network/profile separately from synthetic exercise assumptions; cite local source metadata when available and label missing provenance unknown. Existing `shift-profile-v1.json` explicitly says its duty and timing rules are assumptions, and the current 5 km/h fire-front threshold is a screening choice, not measured physics. Do not invent numeric calibration or legal minima; any new assumed tolerance needs a stated rationale and sensitivity check on development fixtures.

3. **Check runtime invariants in existing audit paths.** Extend `audit_case()` and targeted `IncidentDayEnv` tests around `step`, `_tick`, `_record` and `HazardRouter`: monotonic time and contiguous audit, finite/nonnegative bounded water, fuel and duty, valid state transitions and availability/shift constraints; stock changes tied to permitted refill/work; work only after arrival/setup, in reach and with resources; route direction, current hazard/restriction and safe movement, including explicit emergency-egress exceptions; closures, wind, aircraft daylight/authorization and return reserve as modeled; population and exposure accounting, and deterministic event/order/report causality. Account for valid recovery after shift and censored or unavailable evidence rather than asserting recovery is impossible. Use private audit plus orders/reports where available; mark clearance unsupported if no direct record exists. Preserve `real_map_acceptance.py` wind and route-work-return counterfactual probes and existing safety/censor guards.

4. **Exercise more than one behavior.** Extend the existing `DayDispatcher` / evaluation path with deterministic, versioned scripted policies using only public observations and tools: passive; existing dispatcher; conservative withdrawal/return; and bounded resource/route/refill or evacuation stress. Specify fixed tie-breaking and seeds, actions and expected mechanism coverage, with explicit legitimate no-op/blocked outcomes when a case cannot support a mechanic. Run every policy against every active configuration, reporting intended versus observed mechanics; a completed passive day alone is not evidence that dispatch, closure or recovery paths worked. Reuse `counterfactual_case()` and `wind_event_coverage()` for relevant mechanisms; do not demand drops or invented routes in every case.

5. **Prove interruption equivalence.** Extend `tests/test_incident_day_core.py`, `test_incident_day_runner.py` and `test_incident_day_scenario_coverage_v2.py`: compare uninterrupted versus JSON/disk checkpoint-resumed runs with the same case, policy and seed at representative boundaries (travel, setup/work, refill/return and an event/closure). Compare subsequent actor observations, accepted actions, reports, private audit/trajectory, outcomes and independent rewards, allowing only explicitly nondeterministic metadata such as wall time. Include dispatcher state and reject changed policy, input, network, implementation or corrupted checkpoint via existing `checkpoint`, `restore`, `_save_checkpoint` and `_load_checkpoint` contracts. Mark an unreachable boundary as blocked with evidence, not completed.

6. **Prove the checks can detect faults.** Add small isolated synthetic mutations to `tests/test_incident_day_plausibility.py`, routing and execution tests: too-fast or reversed directed leg, shortened setup/refill, work before arrival or without stock, impossible stock increase, unsafe closure/hazard movement, invalid shift transition, missing audit minute and corrupt/mismatched checkpoint. Assert the specific finding or rejection and a corresponding valid control; test unknown/censored data separately so it is not reported as a contradiction or success. Do not mutate pinned catalogs, archived results or source data.

## Staged CPU acceptance

1. Run targeted lifecycle, routing, plausibility, execution, checkpoint and fault-injection tests first with `python -m pytest` and fix actual failures. Confirm the manifest detects deliberately omitted cells and preserves blocked/not-run statuses.
2. Run one fixture smoke per scripted policy, including an interrupted/resumed episode; then run the complete 6-case fixture × policy matrix. Capture runtime and resource use, findings, mechanic coverage and replay/reward results. Stop and diagnose contradictions or replay mismatches before expanding.
3. Run the same complete matrix on the six pinned real-map development cases through the existing plausibility evaluator with its explicit input/network and approval settings. Use a fresh private output directory, one CPU worker and bounded wall budgets; preserve partial reports and resume only through supported checkpoint paths. The existing plausibility CLI has a wall budget but does not establish resumable matrix execution; implement that integration before claiming it. Report each blocked or not-run cell, including unavailable evidence, and require human review of real-map constraint evidence.
4. Accept the development gate only when every required cell is complete, replay and reward agree, relevant invariants and fault controls pass, no unexplained contradiction or blocker remains, and review candidates/unsupported assumptions are visible. This is exercise consistency, not physical validation or authorization to train. Archive new reports with all pins and keep private artifacts private.

After this gate, prioritize source-backed operational and physical calibration, independent data and an appropriately separated evaluation design before drawing real-world or training claims. Wider generation, performance optimization, GPU training and publication can wait. Preserve archived two-hour IDs, splits, seeds, scores and replay contracts unchanged; version any behavioral change separately. No paid inference, GPU, large generation, publication, external posting, commits or deletion is part of this plan.
