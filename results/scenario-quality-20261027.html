What needs review

  • 4 variants (2 configurations) ignite a refuge at time zero. These may be deliberate unsafe-evacuation challenges, but need explicit intent and domain review.
  • 80 cells share an assumed scrub fuel fallback. This is one landscape gap, repeated in the three catalogs' warning counts.

There are zero hard contradictions under the declared rules; this is not empirical realism certification. 32 variants need case-level review. Existing scenarios and rewards are unchanged.

Unresolved fidelity limits

  • Synthetic normalized wind, spread/suppression and smoke-dose effects lack empirical calibration
  • Off-map bases and assumed ground transit do not establish road/speed realism
  • Coastal weather is context, not observed fire-front weather or coupled physical forcing
  • No policy solvability/difficulty claims; static approval is not a realistic-release certification
