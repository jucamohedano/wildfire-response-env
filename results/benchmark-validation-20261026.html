Findings

  • The dispatcher protected everyone on12/12development cases; removing incoming reports, calls and smoke telemetry reduced that to10/12. Sensor evidence and operational acknowledgements remained.
  • Blanket evacuation is not universally safe: a development route-fire probe exposed200simulated people under indiscriminate evacuation, while informed dispatch avoided exposure.
  • The validation-leading dispatcher reached28/40safe test episodes; cooperative response reached29/40. The more elaborate heuristic is not uniformly better. Test results were not used to retune it.
  • All282compiled ignition events lie on burnable cells for the pinned inputs.

Learning preparation and remaining gates

Three replay-verified training-only trajectories yielded180frozen public retrieval cases. A fresh wheel installation completed and independently verified a120-minute episode. OpenEnv WebSocket parity, unknown-state rendering and bounded historical queries are tested.

Qwen3-1.7B SFT, adapter validation and GRPO are implemented behind explicit compute/budget gates. Actual GPU execution and learned-policy improvement are untested. Code-license and asset review are still required before publication.
