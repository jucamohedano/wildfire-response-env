Missingness is part of the environment

  • Spanish geographic sample: nine records combine zero temperature/wind with absent humidity. Flag for interpretation; do not assume calm weather.
  • US sample: NUM_EVACUATED missing in 64/213 reports; EVACUATION_IN_PROGRESS missing throughout; timezone missing in eight.
  • Resource categories are not initial fleet availability. Missing aircraft arrival is not proof that no aircraft participated.
  • Recorded times have no verified public release/receipt timestamps. Neither dataset supplies caller transcripts, readbacks or optimal counterfactual actions.

Recommended scope

Keep Spain as the primary setting. Use EGIF to constrain first-response milestones and resource categories. Use ICS-209 separately to design repeated situation updates and uncertainty handling, not Spanish doctrine.

Next: decode category lists, check chronology and coordinate systems, then create new development tasks for intake, arrival monitoring, escalation and resource/protection coordination. Missing communications and effects remain explicitly synthetic. Historical outcomes must not override changed agent actions.

The samples differ in size, reporting mechanism and jurisdiction. This is a field audit, not evidence that one country's response or policy is superior.
