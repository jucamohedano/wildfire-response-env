# Huelva evidence and synthetic scenarios

The canonical evidence/assumption pack is
[`huelva_regional_v2.json`](../../envs/wildfire_response/core/src/envs/wildfire_response/data/huelva_regional_v2.json).
It is packaged with the core rather than duplicated here. Its reviewed source
facts, raw/text digests, derived geography and scenario assumptions remain distinct.

Facility names/capability classes and CEMS coverage do not establish actual base
coordinates, inventories, historical ignitions, dispatches, road travel or measured
suppression. Scenarios and reports are synthetic. See the
[regional evidence documentation](../../envs/wildfire_response/core/src/envs/wildfire_response/regional/README.md).
Raw source snapshots/checkpoints are not migrated into this repository.
